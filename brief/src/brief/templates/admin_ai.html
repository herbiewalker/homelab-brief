MCP clients

Outside AI clients (Home Assistant, Claude, others) reach read-only tools at /mcp over HTTPS with a bearer token. Needs the master switch and the mcp_server feature on. A token is shown once, stored only as a hash, and every call is in the audit log. The default scope has no per-device activity, DNS data, or raw log lines; a cloud client can never have those, and what it receives is scanned for addresses and private names.
